How it works

No LLM calls. No prompts. No guessing.

No AI reads your documents

Extraction is plain code. Your PDF is never sent to a language model or any other AI service, so there is nothing to prompt, no API keys to bring and no per-page model bill.

Each form is mapped ahead of time

Every form in the library is mapped once, field by field: where each answer sits, what it is called and what kind of value it holds.

No third parties

Reading a form makes no outside calls: no AI provider, no external API. Your document is read where Petey runs and is not passed along to anyone else.

Same PDF, same answer

No model means no drift between runs. A form takes about a second, and reading it twice gives you the same data twice.
