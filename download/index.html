In the app

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Drag-and-drop extraction
Drop one PDF or a whole folder. Pick a blueprint, get a spreadsheet. No code, no setup.
Blueprint editor
Build, tune, and reuse blueprints inside the app. Start from a starter set or write your own.
Local parsers bundled
PyMuPDF, pdfplumber, and Tesseract come in the box. No extra installs.
Bring your own AI
OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or fully local via Ollama. Use the keys you already have.
CSV & JSON export
Open results in your spreadsheet, your warehouse, or wherever your pipeline picks up.
Runs offline
With a local model, the entire pipeline works without a network connection. Your documents never leave the machine.
