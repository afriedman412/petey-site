How it works

Three steps.
One spreadsheet.

01
Write or pick a schema
Describe the fields you want in plain YAML. Or pull a prebuilt schema from the library — CMS-1500, FEC filings, court dockets, more.
02
Drop in your PDFs
One file or a thousand. Petey wires the parser of your choice to the LLM of your choice and runs everything in parallel.
03
Get a CSV or JSON
Structured rows, ready for your spreadsheet, your warehouse, your RAG pipeline, or whatever comes next.

About

Hi, I'm Adam.

I've spent years pulling data out of PDFs — for journalism, for research, for clients. Every time I started a new project, I built the same pipeline from scratch. Parser, prompt, glue code, output. Throw away. Repeat.

The pieces are all there. Open-source parsers are reliable. Small LLMs can run on a laptop. Per-document cost is pennies. The only missing piece is a tool that wires them together and lets you describe what you want.

That's Petey. LMK what you think — and if you've got a document type you can't crack, send it over.
